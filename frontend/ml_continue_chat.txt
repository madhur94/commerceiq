Step 8 — What happens after detector selection
Even if Exp-B improves mAP, M9 still isn't automatically finished.

The selected detector then goes through:

Selected detector
        ↓
BoT-SORT / ByteTrack
        ↓
continuous trajectories
        ↓
8-frame observation windows
        ↓
Dynamic Scene Graph
        ↓
DriftGraph predictor
Our key downstream metric becomes:

How many valid 8-frame prediction windows are recovered?

This matters because a detector with somewhat better mAP but fragmented detections may still be poor for our temporal prediction system.

Only after that do we freeze the final M9 detector and run the broader TEST evaluation.
